# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 23 Sep 2026. For information only; this is not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures and supplies wires and cables. For Q1 FY27, revenue from operations reached ₹1,248 crore, marking an 18.0 percent increase year-on-year [S3]. Profit after tax (PAT [net profit after taxes]) for the quarter was ₹82 crore, up 5.9 percent from the previous year [S3]. The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 [S3].

## 2. Bull case
- Strong order book growth reached ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, compared to ₹3,150 crore a year earlier [S3].
- Export revenue share increased to 21 percent of total revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16 percent in Q1 FY26 [S3].
- Foreign institutional investor (FII [foreign investors investing in Indian markets]) holding rose to 11.8 percent as of 30 June 2026, compared to 10.9 percent in March 2026 [S8].
- Management guides for full-year FY27 revenue growth of 15 to 17 percent [S2].

## 3. Bear case
- EBITDA margin (operating profit as a share of revenue) contracted by 170 basis points (bps [hundredths of a percentage point]) to 11.2 percent in Q1 FY27 from 12.9 percent in Q1 FY26 [S3].
- Receivable days (average time taken to collect cash from customers) lengthened to 96 days as of June 2026 from 78 days as of June 2025 [S2].
- The company received a regulatory GST (Goods and Services Tax) demand order of ₹46.3 crore covering FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S7].
- Raw material cost increases put pressure on operating margins, alongside a 14 percent rise in raw material expenses [S1].
