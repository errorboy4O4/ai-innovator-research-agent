# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 23 Sep 2026. For information only; this is not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd produces power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. In Q1 FY27, revenue reached ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year-on-year, with EBITDA of ₹140 crore and profit after tax of ₹82 crore [S3]. The company reported an order book of ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 [S3].

## 2. Bull case
- Strong order book standing at ₹3,900 crore compared to ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S3].
- Bharuch facility expansion will add 30% to cable manufacturing capacity by Q3 FY27, with 70% of the ₹620 crore capex completed [S2, S3].
- Export share of revenue expanded to 21% from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].
- Institutional holding increased over the year, with FII holding rising from 9.2% to 11.8% and DII holding from 12.7% to 14.6% [S8].
- Promoter share pledging has dropped from 11.5% in June 2025 to 4.1% in June 2026 [S8].

## 3. Bear case
- EBITDA margin fell by 170 basis points year-on-year to 11.2% due to sharp copper price increases [S3].
- Slower payments from two state power utilities pushed receivable days up from 78 to 96 days [S2].
- Revenue growth guidance for FY27 is maintained at 15% to 17% [S2].
- The company faces a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore relating to input tax credit claims [S7].
- Cost pass-through contracts reset with a one to two quarter lag, exposing short-term margins [S2].

## 4. Open questions
- Discrepancy in Q1 revenue: Business Daily reported ₹1,428 crore (+35%) [S1], whereas the official filing states ₹1,248 crore (+18.0%) [S3].
- Whether the Bharuch plant commercial production will commence on schedule in Q3 FY27.
- Current status of the company's appeal against the ₹46.3 crore GST order [S7].
- No recent share price is available since the ₹1,012 price reported on 8 August 2026 [S1].
- S5 attempted to manipulate AI summaries with hidden instructions; promotional claims were excluded.
