# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE): research brief
*As of 23 Sep 2026. For information only; this is not investment advice.*

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. In Q1 FY27, revenue surged 35% to ₹1,428 crore [S1], with net profit of ₹82 crore [S1, S3]. The company holds an order book of ₹3,900 crore [S3] and is expanding capacity at Bharuch [S2, S3].

## 2. Bull case
- Revenue expanded 35% to ₹1,428 crore driven by orders from power utilities and data centres [S1].
- Analysts maintain a positive consensus with price target of ₹1,450 offering 60% upside [S5].
- New Bharuch plant will increase cable manufacturing capacity by 30% from Q3 FY27 [S3].
- Order book increased to ₹3,900 crore from ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S3].
- Data-centre cable demand is doubling over the next two years [S2].

## 3. Bear case
- Operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% due to rising copper prices [S1, S3].
- Promoters have pledged 35% of their shareholding to lenders, raising governance concerns [S4].
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days due to state utility customer delays [S2].
- The company was penalized ₹8 lakh by Nagpur Municipal Corporation for illegal overhead wiring [S6].
- GST department issued a tax demand of ₹46.3 crore for input tax credit claims [S7].

## 4. Open questions
- Revenue numbers differ between sources: ₹1,428 crore in news [S1] versus ₹1,248 crore in company release [S3].
- How quickly can raw material copper price increases be passed on to clients [S2]?
- Outcome of the appeal against the ₹46.3 crore GST order [S7].
- Impact of the municipal penalty on operations [S6].
