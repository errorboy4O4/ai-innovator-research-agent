# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)
**Date:** 23 September 2026 | **Sector:** Electrical Equipment / Cables

---

### Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. The stock trades at ₹1,012 on the NSE [S1]. 
* **Financial Performance:** Q1 FY27 revenue reached ₹1,248 crore (up 18% YoY per official release; a separate news report cited ₹1,428 crore) [S1, S3]. PAT rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore [S3]. EBITDA margins contracted by 170 bps YoY to 11.2% due to a 14% spike in raw material copper costs [S1, S3].
* **Balance Sheet & Capacity:** Net debt-to-equity is conservative at 0.35x [S2]. The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant (₹620 crore total capex, 70% spent) is set to expand cable capacity by 30% in Q3 FY27 [S2, S3]. 

### Bull Case
* **Secular Demand Tailwinds:** Strong order book of ₹3,900 crore (up 24% YoY) is backed by robust power utility spending and data-centre development [S1, S3]. Data-centre cables currently comprise 9% of domestic revenue and are projected to double within two years [S2].
* **Margin Recovery & Capacity Expansion:** Management expects to pass on surging copper costs to customers over the next one to two quarters via price-variation clauses present in roughly two-thirds of its contracts [S2, S3]. Commercial production at the Bharuch facility in Q3 FY27 will unlock a 30% volume boost [S3].
* **Institutional Confidence:** FII and DII shareholding increased to 11.8% and 14.6% respectively as of June 2026 [S8]. Notably, promoter share pledging dropped significantly to 4.1% down from 11.5% a year prior [S8]. 

### Bear Case
* **Working Capital Strains:** Receivable days lengthened from 78 to 96 days over the past year, driven by slower payments from two major state utility customers [S2]. 
* **Regulatory & Tax Overhangs:** The company received a substantial CGST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) regarding input tax credits for FY20-22, which management is contesting [S7]. *(Note: Local media reports regarding municipal penalties for unauthorized overhead wiring apply to an unrelated local cable TV/broadband entity, Sarvottam Cable Network, rather than Sarvottam Cables Ltd) [S6].*
* **Raw Material Volatility:** Operating margins remain sensitive to copper price fluctuations, leading to immediate profit compression given the 1–2 quarter lag in contract price resets [S1, S2].

### Open Questions
1. **Timing of Pass-Through:** Will upcoming quarters successfully absorb the full extent of high copper prices without damaging order volume?
2. **Cash Flow Impact:** How long will state utility payment delays persist, and will receivable days stabilize or continue to expand?
3. **Tax Liability Outcome:** What is the legal probability and timeline of resolving the ₹46.3 crore CGST demand without a cash outflow?
